# Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Outatime Switcher doesn't collect, store or send any personal information. Everything it does stays on your PC.

## Keyboard input

To notice when you tap Alt or the Windows key on its own, Outatime Switcher watches keyboard input while it runs. It only looks at whether those keys were pressed and released on their own. It doesn't record, store or send any key you type.

## Files on your PC

Outatime Switcher keeps these files on your PC and never sends them anywhere:

- **Settings**: Your choices in the settings app, in `settings.json`.
- **Diagnostics log**: When it can't switch the IME, the time, the name of the app in front, and the IME's state, in `diagnostics.log`. It never contains what you type, and stays under 128 KB.

To remove them, uninstall the app. For the zip version, also delete the `Outatime` folders in `%APPDATA%` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%`.

## Network

Outatime Switcher doesn't connect to the internet.

## Microsoft Store

If you install it from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect data about the installation and about crashes, under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published in this file, with a new effective date.
